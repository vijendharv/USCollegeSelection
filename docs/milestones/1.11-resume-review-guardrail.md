# Milestone 1.11 — Résumé evidence review guardrail

**Status:** Complete

**Implementation pull request:** Pending

## Objective

Prevent résumé evidence from being silently ignored in free-form notes or silently scored before user review. Establish the structured review lifecycle that the later PDF parser and confirmation UI must use.

## Delivered behavior

- `HolisticProfile` has an explicit `review_status` with `needs_review` and `confirmed` values.
- Parsed or manually staged résumé activities and themes can remain structured while awaiting confirmation.
- Profile assessment emits `holistic_confirmation_required` when holistic evidence needs review.
- Analysis remains available, but unconfirmed résumé evidence is excluded from holistic scoring.
- The Excel Student Profile sheet displays the résumé evidence review status.
- Confirmed structured themes and activities continue to participate only in holistic alignment and cannot change Safety / Likely, Target, Reach, or Insufficient Data classification.
- The scoring methodology and product requirements define the parser-to-confirmation contract.

## Review lifecycle

```text
PDF or manual extraction
        |
        v
structured activities and themes
review_status = needs_review
        |
        v
user reviews and edits evidence
        |
        v
review_status = confirmed
        |
        v
eligible for holistic alignment scoring
```

Free-form academic notes are never interpreted as résumé evidence by the ranker.
