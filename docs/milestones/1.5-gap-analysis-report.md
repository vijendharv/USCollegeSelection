# Milestone 1.5 — Gap analysis and report model

**Status:** Complete

**Completed:** 2026-07-01

**Pull request:** [#9 Implement gap analysis report model](https://github.com/vijendharv/USCollegeSelection/pull/9)

## Objective

Create one canonical, reproducible college report used unchanged by the future on-screen view, PDF, and Excel exports. Combine classifications with academic and cost comparisons while making strengths, gaps, missing data, warnings, and sources explicit for every school.

## Delivered

### Canonical report contract

`CollegeReport` contains:

- report and classification-methodology versions;
- generation timestamp and dataset provenance;
- the confirmed student profile;
- confirmed résumé/activity themes supplied as holistic context;
- every school report; and
- a standard planning disclaimer.

Each `SchoolReport` carries the full institution record, user-entered flag, unchanged classification result, comparison rows, strengths, gaps, unknowns, warnings, suggested actions, and deduplicated source references. Future renderers do not recalculate values.

### Academic gap rows

Compatible GPA, SAT, and ACT classification rules become comparison rows containing:

- student value;
- school benchmark range;
- numeric distance from the nearest range boundary;
- strength, competitive, or gap status;
- explanatory note; and
- source references.

Confirmed AP, Honors, IB, and dual-enrollment counts remain separate from GPA. Until an institution rigor benchmark exists, the report labels that comparison unknown rather than manufacturing a gap.

### Cost comparison

Cost respects the meaning selected with the optional annual budget:

- net-price budget uses published average net price and warns that it is not student-specific;
- published-cost budget prefers total cost of attendance and falls back to appropriate public in-state or out-of-state tuition;
- out-of-pocket budget remains unknown because average institutional prices are not a family-specific estimate; and
- missing budget leaves cost visible and unscored.

An over-budget result produces a warning but never changes the admissions classification.

### Inclusion and preference warnings

Candidates are deduplicated by IPEDS UNITID. A user-entered candidate wins over a duplicate discovered candidate and is always retained. Excluded-state and outside-preferred-state conflicts become visible warnings instead of silently removing the school.

### Missing data and holistic context

Classification `missing_inputs` become report unknowns. Incompatible or intentionally unused evidence remains a warning, preserving the user-facing distinction established in Milestone 1.4. Confirmed résumé themes, strengths, and opportunities can be carried in `HolisticContext`, but do not alter the academic classification.

## Design decisions

- Keep report assembly pure and independent of networking, DuckDB, and output libraries.
- Preserve the classification result instead of duplicating its logic.
- Require the caller to provide `generated_at` so fixtures and regenerated reports are reproducible.
- Use numeric gaps only for genuinely compatible values.
- Never treat average net price as a student-specific aid estimate.
- Keep résumé-derived context descriptive and separate from admissions classification.
- Retain user-entered schools even when they conflict with filters.

## How to use it

```python
from app.reporting import build_college_report

report = build_college_report(
    student_profile,
    candidates,
    dataset_version,
    generated_at=generation_time,
    holistic_context=confirmed_resume_context,
)

for school in report.schools:
    print(school.institution.name, school.classification.category)
    print("Missing data:", school.unknowns)
```
