# Milestone 1.14 — PDF detail ordering and navigation

**Status:** Complete

**Implementation pull request:** Pending

## Objective

Make a long college report easy to traverse and ensure school-detail pages follow the same sequence families see in the recommendation tables.

## Delivered behavior

- School details follow first appearance in the student-supplied and generated recommendation tables.
- A school included for multiple majors appears once at its earliest position.
- A clickable report-navigation menu links to recommendation tables, every school detail, and the addendum when present.
- Every school-detail section includes a return-to-menu link.
- Schools without a ranking row remain included after ranked schools in canonical report order.

## Verification

- Focused PDF tests verify internal destinations and deterministic ordering.
- A rendered PDF confirms readable menu and detail-page layout.
