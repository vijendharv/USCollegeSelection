# Milestone 1.15 — Policy-aware, state-aware recommendations

## Outcome

The recommendation pipeline now treats the student's residence state and documented school policy as first-class evidence. It also calculates reviewable academic summaries and preparation signals without presenting them as school-reported GPAs or major-specific admit rates.

## What changed

- Added sourced enums and institution fields for test policy, residency selectivity, and major-admission structure.
- Added a checked-in override file and loader. Unknown institutions remain unknown; the loader does not infer policy from name, acceptance rate, or ownership.
- Excluded tests at test-blind and primary-review-hidden schools. At optional schools, undecided scores are excluded. ACT/SAT-only evidence can support Target or Reach but cannot create Safety / Likely.
- Used `residence_state` to label in-state public context and to downgrade one category for sourced high/very-high nonresident selectivity. No penalty is applied when policy is unknown.
- Added a Washington regional baseline controlled by `recommendation_settings.include_regional_baseline`, defaulting to true. Regional schools are labeled and retained for evaluation, not guaranteed or promoted ahead of qualified recommendations.
- Added cumulative, core, STEM, grades 10-11, internal weighted, UC unweighted, and UC capped-weighted calculations. UC variants require confirmed A-G and honors-eligibility data.
- Added grade-trend and life-science preparation signals. These remain report context and do not silently change admission categories.
- Added threshold-relaxation warnings and policy-, residency-, major-, cost-, and pre-health-aware actions.

## Deliberate non-changes

- The product keeps Safety / Likely, Target, Reach, and Insufficient Data; it does not add a Far Reach category.
- Adaptive 80-to-70 thresholds remain the default because they are user-configurable and category-specific. Relaxation is now prominent.
- The ranking keeps the agreed fit-qualified pool followed by program-strength ordering. The feedback's suggestion to reverse that order was not adopted.
- Six-digit CIP confirms exact program availability but does not prove that a major is capped or direct-admit. Those claims require a sourced policy override.

## Verification

The focused regression suite covers test policies, ACT-only classification, out-of-state public-school adjustments, GPA variants, preparation signals, and policy loading. Ruff and the complete automated suite must pass before merge.

## Policy maintenance

Institution policies change more often than federal outcome data. Each override must include an official URL and review date. Add or change a policy only after checking the institution or system's official admissions page.
